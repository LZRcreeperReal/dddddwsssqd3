# Song → Brazilian Funk (automotivo / montagem)
Outside Brazil this often gets called "Brazilian phonk." Automotivo = made for high-power car sound systems, which is why it's in car edits.

## Styles
| style | BPM | feel |
|---|---|---|
| Tamborzão / funk carioca | ~130 | syncopated kick-clap groove, swing |
| Automotivo / mandelão | 130–150 | heavy, repetitive, four-on-the-floor, huge sub, made for car audio |
| Montagem | ~130 | looped vocal chops (often pitched), synth/flute melody, distorted bass |
| Funk 150 | 150 | faster, tight notes, forward push |
| Brazilian phonk (fusion) | 130–160 | funk groove + phonk cowbell/808 |

## Target
- **Groove:** tamborzão kick-clap (#69) with 55–60% swing on the 1/16s, or straight 4/4 for automotivo
- **Bass:** overdriven sub, short "tuim" hits (#78), bass-test sweeps for automotivo (#82)
- **Vocal:** chopped and looped as a rhythm layer, often pitched up (chipmunk #85) or way down
- **Melody:** short repetitive synth/flute/cowbell riff (detuned cowbell #58 for brazilian phonk)
- **Space:** don't crowd the percussion, the groove needs room

## Conversion steps
1. Analyze BPM + key, split stems
2. Retime to 130 (montagem/tamborzão) or 150 (funk 150/automotivo)
3. **Vocal:** pick the catchiest 1–2 bar hook, chop into slices, re-sequence them on the groove (call-and-response). Pitch up +5 to +8 st for the montagem sound, or keep it
4. **Melody:** loop the song's best 2–4 note riff, replay on detuned cowbell, flute or synth
5. **Drums:** tamborzão loop or 4/4 automotivo kick, claps, surdo, shaker with swing
6. **Bass:** distorted sub on the kicks, tuim hits on accents
7. **SFX:** bass-test sweep intro, riser + silence → drop, car sfx on accents
8. **Master:** very loud, bass-heavy (car audio), saturation on the sub

## Phonk vs Funk quick difference
| | drift phonk | brazilian funk |
|---|---|---|
| groove | trap/half-time + 1/16 hats | tamborzão syncopation or 4/4, swing |
| melody | distorted cowbell | vocal chops + short riff |
| vocal | Memphis chops, screwed | Portuguese MC chops, pitched up |
| bpm | 140–160 | 130 / 150 |
