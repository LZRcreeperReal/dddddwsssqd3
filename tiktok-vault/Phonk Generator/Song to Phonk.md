# Song → Drift Phonk
Drift phonk = the car-edit phonk: fast, distorted cowbell melody, clipped 808s, dark mood.

## Target
- **BPM:** 140–160 (some sit half-time ~70–80 feel)
- **Key:** minor. If the song's major, keep the vocal and write the cowbell in its relative minor
- **Core:** distorted 808 cowbell melody, distorted gliding 808, punchy kick/clap, 1/16 hats, dark atmos, sparse vocal chops
- **Structure:** sparse intro (808 + chop) → full drums + cowbell on the first big hit

## Conversion steps
1. **Analyze** BPM + key (librosa)
2. **Split stems** (demucs): vocals / drums / bass / other
3. **Retime** to 140–160. If the song is ~70–80 BPM, double-time it. If 100–120, speed up (sped-up edits are normal in car edits) or time-stretch to keep pitch
4. **Screw the vocal (optional):** pitch −3 to −5 semitones for the Memphis feel, or keep it
5. **Chop:** take 1–2 short vocal hooks, loop them every 2 bars. Don't use the full verse
6. **Melody → cowbell:** take the song's main melody (from "other" stem → pitch track) and replay it on a pitched 808 cowbell (#56), distorted
7. **Drums:** replace the original drums with a phonk kit (kick, clipped snare/clap on 3 in half-time, 1/16 hats with rolls)
8. **Bass:** 808 following the root notes, glides between notes, distortion
9. **SFX:** follow the placement cheat sheet in [[SFX Library]]
10. **Master:** saturation + clip (loud, a bit crunchy), sidechain everything to the kick, light vinyl/tape texture

## What makes it sound "phonk" (in order of importance)
1. Distorted cowbell melody
2. Clipped, distorted 808
3. Tempo 140–160
4. Memphis vocal chops / screwed vocals
5. Lo-fi texture (crackle, tape)
