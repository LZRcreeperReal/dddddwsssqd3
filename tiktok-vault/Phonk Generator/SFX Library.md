## Placement cheat sheet (car edits)
- **Intro (0–3s):** crackle/tape start + muffled beat + door/ignition on beat 1
- **Build:** riser + turbo spool + snare roll, last beat = silence
- **Drop:** 808 boom + crash + distorted cowbell, screech on the first drift frame
- **During drift:** screech ducked under the beat, exhaust pops replace hats, BOV on beat 4
- **Cuts:** whoosh on whip pans, stutter on shakes, tape stop when the car stops
- Don't stack more than 2 sfx on one hit. Sfx sit ~6dB under the beat except the drop
